01

The meaning behind the name

Aristotle named three ways of being human. We took the one that ships.

θεωρία
Theoria · to think

Contemplation. Knowledge pursued for its own end — truth. The plan in your head before context resets it.

ποίησις
Poiesis · to make

Production. Activity valued for the artifact it leaves behind. Useful, but means to a separate end.

πρᾶξις
Praxis · to do

Action guided by practical wisdom — phronesis. Its end is the right act itself, done well, again and again.

For Aristotle, praxis was the practical, public life — doing directed toward the right ordering of the world, guided by phronesis, the practical wisdom to choose well. The Greeks even had a word for doing it well: eupraxia, εὐπραξία — good practice.

That is the entire premise of the product. You author theory in friendly Markdown. Praxis compiles it into machine-readable state, enforces a disciplined lifecycle, and turns intention into action that no reset can erase.

The philosophers have only interpreted the world; the point is to change it.— Marx, on praxis as theory and action, each shaping the other

Praxis is the bridge between knowing and doing — a loop, not a line. Reflection sharpens action; action is written back as new state; the cycle never drifts.

02

The mark

Π — the Greek capital Pi. Two uprights and a lintel: a gate. The threshold theory crosses to become action.

PRAXIS
  • Built on a 6-unit grid. The lintel spans the full width and overhangs the legs — the classical proportion of the letter Π.
  • The right leg carries the accent. One upright in terracotta marks the step from theory (ink) into action (accent). Never both legs.
  • Equal stroke weight across lintel and legs keeps the mark legible from 16px to billboard.
  • The negative space is a doorway — the gate metaphor is intentional. Keep it open; never fill it.

Color

Warm ink on bone, the color of aged manuscript and graphite. Terracotta is the single voice of action; ochre supports.

Accents are oklch-harmonized — equal chroma and lightness, hue apart. Terracotta marks action: the live leg of the mark, links, the one button that matters. Ochre is reserved for state and status — KPIs, badges, "in progress."

06

Voice & messaging

Authoritative, precise, and a little classical. Praxis states standards; it doesn't plead. Short sentences. Earned confidence.

✓ We sound like

  • Disciplined and declarative — "State the goal in one sentence."
  • Plainspoken about rigor — "Hard release gates. No exceptions."
  • Quietly classical — a Greek term, used precisely, never decoratively.
  • Builder-to-builder — we respect the reader's craft.

✕ We never sound like

  • Hype-driven — no "revolutionary," "10x," "game-changing."
  • Cute or emoji-laden — the rigor is the personality.
  • Vague — never "praxis" as a buzzword without its meaning.
  • Apologetic or hedging — the system has opinions.
